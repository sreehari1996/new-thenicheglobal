Our Kochi Story

In 2019, we started our training and capability development centre in Kochi, Kerala, to strengthen and upgrade the expertise of top-notch software professionals and niche consultants for global projects.

Located in the Vismaya Building at Infopark Phase 1, Kakkanad, Kochi, we operate as a fully functional engineering, training, and delivery centre supporting global clients in various technology workspaces.

Ever since its inception, our Kochi unit has emerged as a strategic centre to elevate our training capabilities for a future-ready talent force. What we train and develop in Kochi is prepped and strengthened for the global platform.
