The Powerhouse

Our Kochi Story

For us, Kochi is more than an office location. It is home.

The Niche story has deep Malayalee roots. Members of our leadership made their own journey from Kerala to the United States, built careers there, and experienced firsthand what it takes to establish a life and career in a new country. That journey took some of us all the way to Silicon Valley and leadership positions in corporate America, including serving as a Vice President at one of the world's leading banks.

But whenever we came back home, we noticed something. In Kerala, there is enormous global ambition. Yet when it comes to opportunities in the United States, there is also a great deal of confusion. People often hear conflicting information about education, careers, visas, sponsorship, costs, and life after moving abroad. Families can end up making some of the biggest decisions of their lives without having a clear picture of the options available to them.

We wanted to change that.

Niche Global was created to bring the experience, exposure, and international network we built over the years closer to home. We are not here to tell everyone to leave Kerala. We are here to help people understand their options, evaluate what is realistic for their profile, and make an informed decision about their global transition.

Because moving to another country should never begin with a sales pitch. It should begin with clarity.
