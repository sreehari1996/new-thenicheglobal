CAREER PATHWAY

International Internships

Kickstart your global career early. Gain practical, hands-on experience with international firms, building a resume that stands out worldwide.

Practical Experience

Apply your theoretical knowledge in real-world corporate environments alongside industry experts.

Cultural Immersion

Learn global business etiquette and cross-cultural communication skills highly valued by multinational companies.

Conversion Potential

Many international internships act as extended interviews, frequently resulting in full-time job offers upon completion.

THE PROCESS

How the Internship Pathway Works

1

Eligibility & Placement Matching

We evaluate your academic background (students and recent graduates) and match you with short-term placement opportunities in your chosen field.

2

Interviews & Documentation

We help you prepare for host company interviews. Once accepted, we assist with the specific exchange/internship visa documentation required (e.g., J-1 in the USA).

3

The Internship Experience

Relocate for 6 to 12 months to work directly with the host company. You gain invaluable skills while receiving mentorship and supervision.

4

Return or Transition

Return home with a globally competitive resume, or, if eligible and offered, transition your internship visa into a sponsored employment visa.
