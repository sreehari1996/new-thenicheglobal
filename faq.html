General Inquiries

What exactly does Niche Global do?

Niche Global is an international career pathway consultancy. We help skilled professionals — nurses, engineers, hospitality workers, and more — identify and navigate realistic, verified routes to build a career and life in another country. We are not a recruitment agency; we are a structured advisory and placement ecosystem built on verified relationships with employers, institutions, and credentialing bodies.

Is Niche Global a recruitment agency?

No. A traditional recruitment agency fills a vacancy for an employer. We work differently — we work for the candidate. Our goal is to build a complete pathway: from profile assessment and skills bridging, to employer matching, visa guidance, and post-arrival support. We are selective about the employers and institutions we work with, and we only represent relationships we have independently verified.

Who is Niche Global's ideal candidate?

Our ideal candidate is a qualified professional — typically a registered nurse, civil engineer, software developer, accountant, or hospitality specialist — who has at least 2–3 years of post-qualification experience and is serious about building a career internationally, not just finding a temporary job abroad. We work best with people who are proactive, willing to invest in upskilling where required, and who value a structured, transparent process over quick-fix promises.

How does Niche Global make money? What are your fees?

We operate on a transparent, service-based fee structure. Depending on the complexity of your pathway, there may be a consultation fee, a pathway management fee, and/or a placement fee. We will always disclose every cost clearly before you commit to anything. We do not take hidden commissions from employers at your expense. Our business model depends on successful, long-term placements — so our incentive is always your success.

Pathways & Visas

Which countries do you facilitate pathways to?

Currently, our active pathways are in the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, and the UAE. We are actively developing pathways to the Netherlands, Ireland, and New Zealand. The availability of a pathway depends on your profession, your qualifications, and your eligibility under that country's immigration rules at a given time. Not every pathway is open to every candidate — this is something we assess during your initial consultation.

Do you guarantee a visa or job placement?

No — and we will never make that promise. Any consultancy that "guarantees" a visa or job abroad should be treated with extreme caution. Immigration decisions are made by sovereign governments, not by consultancies. What we do guarantee is a structured, honest process; access to our verified employer and institutional network; and a transparent assessment of your realistic chances before you invest any money or time. We would rather tell you a pathway isn't right for you than take your money for one that isn't.

How long does the process typically take?

It varies significantly by profession, destination, and how quickly you complete your side of the process. A straightforward technology pathway to Canada or the UAE can be as short as 3–6 months. A healthcare pathway to the UK — which involves a licensing examination, skills assessment, and regulatory registration — can take 12–24 months. We will give you a realistic, profession-specific timeline estimate during your initial assessment.

Will I need to retrain or pass exams abroad?

For regulated professions (healthcare, engineering, law, finance), the destination country's regulatory body will assess your qualifications. In some cases, a bridging exam, additional training, or a supervised practice period is required. This is not a negative — it is the legitimate entry standard that ensures you are positioned properly for long-term career success in that country. We help you prepare for these requirements through our verified training provider network.

Working With Us

How do I get started with Niche Global?

Start with our Profile Matcher tool on the homepage. It takes about 5 minutes and gives us enough context to understand your profile and identify which pathways might be relevant. We will follow up with a complimentary introductory consultation — a real conversation with a pathway specialist, not an automated email sequence — to discuss your options in detail.

Can my family come with me?

In most cases, yes. Many of the work visa routes we facilitate include dependent visa provisions for spouses and children. The specific rules vary by country and visa category. We factor family context into your pathway planning from the start, including considerations like schooling availability, spousal work rights, and family healthcare access in the destination country.

What support do you provide after I arrive?

Our support does not end when you board the flight. We provide a structured post-arrival check-in during your first 3 months, connecting you to our community of Niche Global alumni in your destination city where available. We also assist with initial orientation — banking setup, NHS/Medicare registration, local SIM, and other essential first steps. Long-term, we remain a point of contact for career progression questions and future pathway planning (e.g., permanent residency).

I was told a particular route is closed. Can Niche Global still help?

It depends. Immigration routes open and close frequently, and information circulating in online communities is often out of date or misunderstood. We track policy changes across our active destination countries in real time. Sometimes what appears to be a "closed" route is simply one that requires a different employer type, a different sponsorship tier, or a slightly different credential framing. Contact us — we will give you an honest assessment based on current rules.
