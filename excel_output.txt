Sheet: Guidelines
Row 1: Niche Global Website — Working Guidelines
Row 2: Document Purpose | Phase 1 working input for the website content writer and designer. Website positioning becomes the master source for future marketing, social media, articles, blogs, brochures, emails and other brand communication. This should evolve into a living reference for what Niche Global is about.
Row 3: Current Positioning | Niche Global is developing an overseas education and global career-pathway proposition focused primarily on people from Kerala. “Global Career Company” remains a working positioning and should be legally/regulatorily validated before formal use.
Row 4: Core Philosophy | Profile first. Career outcome first. Pathway second. Understand the individual before recommending a destination or route.
Row 5: Brand Slogan | Life Beyond Borders
Row 6: Priority Markets | USA | UK | Australia | France | South Korea
Row 7: Core Journey | Person → Career → Destination → Pathway
Row 8: Visual Principle | Real First. AI Enhanced. Never Generic.
Row 9: Content Principle | Don't tell people what Niche Global isn't. Show them what Niche Global is.
Row 10: Pathway Principle | Sell the career and life outcome — not the visa, course or pathway.
Row 11: Trust Principle | Trust should come from how Niche Global operates, not from exaggerated advertising claims.
Row 12: CTA System | Primary conversion: Find My Pathway / Check My Profile. Contextual exploration: Explore [Country], Explore Pathways, Our Story. High-intent conversation: Talk to Us / equivalent. Do not force a CTA into every section.
Row 13: Legal / Compliance | Current activities should be framed around services Niche Global is legally permitted to provide. Direct overseas recruitment, employment placement, work-visa claims, guarantees, partner terminology and outcome claims require validation before publication.
Row 14: Spreadsheet Structure | No merged cells. Repeat parent section names for every component/row so sorting and filtering remain functional. Yellow cells are intended for designer links; green cells for final content-writer copy.
Row 15: Phase 1 Scope | Homepage only for now. Inner pages to be developed later if required.
